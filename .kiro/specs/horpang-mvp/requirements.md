# Requirements Document

## Introduction

Horpang is a gamified haunted quiz web application for teenagers studying for a test through a four-stage horror experience (Title, Gallery, Corridor, Summary). The player first answers every Polaroid question correctly in the Gallery, then hunts for the glowing EXIT door in the dark Corridor with a flashlight, and finally sees the Summary. Educators customize quiz content in an editor called The Grimoire and share quizzes through a URL hash, with no backend. The application is built with TypeScript (strict), Vite, Tailwind CSS and the Web Audio API, and all visuals and sounds are original and generated at runtime. The visual and interaction rules in `.kiro/steering/horpang-aesthetic.md` apply to every requirement.

The requirements are split into two areas:

- **Area A: Quiz Data and URL-Sharing Engine** (Requirements 1 to 6). Pure logic with no DOM dependency.
- **Area B: Interactive Horror UI** (Requirements 7 to 20). Stages, Jump Scare Shuffler, Guidance Controller, Score State Engine, Grimoire, Audio Engine Lantern Mode and the No External Assets rule.

## Glossary

- **Horpang_App**: The complete web application.
- **Quiz_Engine**: The pure module that owns the question model, validation, import, export, and URL encoding and decoding.
- **Question**: A record with a unique id, a prompt, two to six answer options, one correct option index, a study explanation, a study hint, and a concept label.
- **Quiz**: An ordered list of one or more Questions with a quiz title.
- **Validator**: The Quiz_Engine component that checks a Quiz or Question against the question model.
- **JSON_Codec**: The Quiz_Engine component that parses and prints Quizzes as JSON.
- **CSV_Codec**: The Quiz_Engine component that parses and prints Quizzes as CSV.
- **Share_Codec**: The Quiz_Engine component that encodes a Quiz into a lz-string compressed URL hash and decodes it back.
- **Share_Hash**: The lz-string compressed, URL-safe string stored in the URL fragment.
- **Default_Quiz**: The built-in sample Quiz with at least five Questions.
- **Stage_Controller**: The UI component that moves the player among the Title, Corridor, Gallery and Summary stages.
- **Title_Stage**: Stage 1, showing the blood-pool intro and the HORPANG title.
- **Gallery_Stage**: Stage 2, the HUD, Polaroid cards, quiz modal and Score_Footer.
- **Corridor_Stage**: Stage 3, the dark stairwell hallway with the flashlight mask and EXIT door, entered only after all Questions are solved.
- **Blood_Canvas**: The HTML canvas on the Title_Stage that draws the animated blood pools and splatter droplets.
- **Title_Font**: The "Road Rage" display font loaded from the Google_Fonts_Stylesheet, with a system fallback font stack.
- **Google_Fonts_Stylesheet**: The Google Fonts CSS resource that supplies the Title_Font and other display fonts; the only permitted external resource.
- **Cursed_Photograph**: An original inline SVG image generated by the Horpang_App and shown on a Polaroid.
- **Score_Footer**: The persistent score readout at the bottom of the Gallery_Stage that duplicates the HUD score.
- **Summary_Stage**: Stage 4, showing final results.
- **Flashlight_Mask**: The oversized shadow overlay with a radial-gradient transparent center that follows the pointer or touch position.
- **EXIT_Door**: The glowing green (`#39ff14`) door button in the Corridor_Stage; activating it ends the escape.
- **HUD**: The always-visible top bar in the Gallery_Stage, shown only in the Gallery_Stage.
- **Polaroid**: A tilted, monochrome card, implemented as a button, in the Gallery_Stage representing one Question.
- **Quiz_Modal**: The high-contrast dialog that shows one Question and its options.
- **Banished_Seal**: The green seal with the text "Banished" shown on a solved Polaroid.
- **Score_Engine**: The pure state module that tracks score, streak, multiplier, battery, progress, and per-question results.
- **Streak**: The count of consecutive correct answers.
- **Streak_Multiplier**: The integer multiplier applied to points, equal to the lesser of (Streak + 1) and 5.
- **Battery**: The flashlight battery percentage, an integer from 0 to 100.
- **Guidance_Controller**: The module that selects the guide banner text from the current game state.
- **Scare_Shuffler**: The pure module that selects the next jump scare.
- **Jump_Scare**: One of four full-screen CSS/SVG animations: Split-Face Mechanical Endoskeleton, Lunging Shadow Entity, Analog CRT Static and Phantom Face, Shattered Glass and Reaching Shadow Hands.
- **Scare_Renderer**: The UI component that displays a Jump_Scare overlay.
- **Audio_Engine**: The module that synthesizes all sound with the Web Audio API.
- **Lantern_Mode**: The accessibility mode that replaces jump scares and flashing with calm feedback.
- **Lantern_Store**: The component that persists the Lantern_Mode setting in `localStorage`.
- **Grimoire**: The Educator Studio, the in-app editor for Quizzes.
- **Missed_Concept**: The concept label of a Question that the player answered incorrectly at least once.

## Requirements

## Area A: Quiz Data and URL-Sharing Engine

### Requirement 1: Question Model and Validation

**User Story:** As an educator, I want a well-defined question model with validation, so that every quiz the game plays is complete and consistent.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Validator SHALL accept a Question only when the Question has a prompt of 1 to 300 characters, where a prompt consisting only of whitespace characters counts as empty.
2. THE Validator SHALL accept a Question only when the Question has 2 to 6 answer options, and each option is 1 to 120 characters long and is not composed only of whitespace characters.
3. THE Validator SHALL accept a Question only when the correct option index is an integer from 0 to (number of answer options - 1), inclusive.
4. THE Validator SHALL accept a Question only when the Question has a study explanation of 1 to 500 characters, a study hint of 1 to 200 characters, and a concept label of 1 to 50 characters, where a value consisting only of whitespace characters counts as empty.
5. THE Validator SHALL accept a Quiz only when the Quiz has 1 to 30 Questions and a title of 1 to 80 characters, where a title consisting only of whitespace characters counts as empty.
6. IF a Quiz or Question fails validation, THEN THE Validator SHALL reject it without modifying it and SHALL return a list containing one error for every failing field, where each error identifies the 1-based Question position (or the Quiz itself for Quiz-level fields) and the name of the failing field.
7. IF two or more Questions within a Quiz share the same id, THEN THE Validator SHALL reject the Quiz and return one error for each Question after the first that uses the duplicated id, identifying that Question's position and the id field.
8. IF a required Question field or Quiz field is missing or is not of the expected type, THEN THE Validator SHALL reject it and return an error identifying the Question position and the field, without raising an unhandled exception.

### Requirement 2: Default Sample Quiz

**User Story:** As a student, I want a ready-to-play sample quiz, so that I can play immediately without any setup.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Quiz_Engine SHALL provide a Default_Quiz that contains between 5 and 20 Questions, inclusive.
2. THE Default_Quiz SHALL pass the Validator with zero validation errors.
3. WHEN the Horpang_App starts with no Share_Hash in the URL, THE Horpang_App SHALL load the Default_Quiz and make it available to the Gallery_Stage within 2 seconds.
4. IF the Horpang_App starts with a Share_Hash in the URL that is empty, THEN THE Horpang_App SHALL treat the URL as having no Share_Hash and load the Default_Quiz.
5. IF the Default_Quiz fails to load or fails the Validator at startup, THEN THE Horpang_App SHALL display an error message indicating the sample quiz is unavailable and SHALL NOT start a game session.

### Requirement 3: JSON Import and Export

**User Story:** As an educator, I want to import and export quizzes as JSON, so that I can back up and reuse my content.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a JSON document of 1 MB (1,048,576 bytes) or less is provided, THE JSON_Codec SHALL parse the document into a Quiz and pass the Quiz to the Validator.
2. IF the JSON document is not syntactically valid, THEN THE JSON_Codec SHALL return an error list containing at least one error that identifies the line and column of the first syntax error, and SHALL leave the current Quiz unchanged.
3. IF the Validator reports one or more validation errors for the parsed Quiz, THEN THE JSON_Codec SHALL return the complete error list produced by the Validator, and SHALL leave the current Quiz unchanged.
4. WHEN a Quiz is provided, THE JSON_Codec SHALL print the Quiz as a syntactically valid JSON document encoded as UTF-8.
5. FOR ALL valid Quizzes, parsing the printed JSON of the Quiz SHALL produce a Quiz equal to the original, where equality compares every field of the Quiz including the order of its questions and answer options (round-trip property).
6. IF an imported file exceeds 1 MB (1,048,576 bytes), THEN THE JSON_Codec SHALL reject the file with an error indicating the size limit was exceeded, without parsing the file, and SHALL leave the current Quiz unchanged.
7. IF the provided JSON document is empty (0 bytes) or contains only whitespace, THEN THE JSON_Codec SHALL return an error indicating that the document is empty, and SHALL leave the current Quiz unchanged.

### Requirement 4: CSV Import and Export

**User Story:** As an educator, I want to import and export quizzes as CSV, so that I can author questions in a spreadsheet.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE CSV_Codec SHALL use exactly one header row followed by one row per Question, with columns in this order: id, prompt, options, correct option index, explanation, hint, concept.
2. WHEN a CSV document of 1 MB or less is provided, THE CSV_Codec SHALL parse the document into a Quiz and pass the Quiz to the Validator.
3. WHEN a field contains a comma, a double quote or a line break, THE CSV_Codec SHALL print the field enclosed in double quotes with each inner double quote doubled.
4. WHEN a quoted CSV field is parsed, THE CSV_Codec SHALL restore all commas, double quotes and line breaks contained in the field.
5. IF the CSV document has a missing column, THEN THE CSV_Codec SHALL return an error list that contains one entry per missing column, each naming the column and the 1-based row number (the header row is row 1), and SHALL leave the current Quiz unchanged.
6. IF the CSV document contains an unterminated quoted field, THEN THE CSV_Codec SHALL return an error list that names the 1-based row number where the quoted field began, and SHALL leave the current Quiz unchanged.
7. IF the Validator reports that the parsed Quiz fails validation, THEN THE CSV_Codec SHALL return an error list that contains one entry per failing Question, each naming the 1-based row number of that Question, and SHALL leave the current Quiz unchanged.
8. FOR ALL valid Quizzes, parsing the printed CSV of the Quiz SHALL produce a Quiz equal to the original (round-trip property).
9. IF an imported file exceeds 1 MB (1,048,576 bytes), THEN THE CSV_Codec SHALL reject the file before parsing, return an error indicating the file size limit was exceeded, and leave the current Quiz unchanged.

### Requirement 5: URL Sharing

**User Story:** As an educator, I want to share a quiz as a link, so that students and judges can play it without any backend.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a valid Quiz is provided, THE Share_Codec SHALL encode the Quiz into a Share_Hash using lz-string URL-safe compression, producing a string that contains only characters permitted in a URL fragment without percent-encoding.
2. WHEN a Share_Hash of 1 to 8000 characters is provided, THE Share_Codec SHALL decode the Share_Hash into a Quiz and pass the Quiz to the Validator.
3. IF a provided Share_Hash is empty, THEN THE Share_Codec SHALL return a decode error indicating that the Share_Hash is empty, and THE Share_Codec SHALL NOT pass any Quiz to the Validator.
4. IF a provided Share_Hash cannot be decompressed or does not decode to a well-formed Quiz structure, THEN THE Share_Codec SHALL return a decode error indicating the Share_Hash is malformed, and THE Share_Codec SHALL NOT pass any Quiz to the Validator.
5. FOR ALL valid Quizzes whose encoded Share_Hash is 8000 characters or fewer, decoding the encoded Share_Hash of the Quiz SHALL produce a Quiz equal to the original (round-trip property).
6. FOR ALL Quizzes, encoding the Quiz twice SHALL produce identical Share_Hash values (determinism).
7. IF the encoded Share_Hash exceeds 8000 characters, THEN THE Share_Codec SHALL return a size error that states the actual length and the 8000-character limit, and THE Share_Codec SHALL NOT return a Share_Hash.
8. WHEN the Horpang_App starts with a non-empty Share_Hash in the URL fragment, THE Horpang_App SHALL decode the Share_Hash and load the resulting Quiz.
9. IF decoding or validation of the Share_Hash in the URL fragment fails at startup, THEN THE Horpang_App SHALL handle the failure as specified in Requirement 6.

### Requirement 6: Invalid and Oversized Hash Handling

**User Story:** As a student, I want a broken or oversized link to fail safely, so that the game still works.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. IF the URL fragment contains a Share_Hash that cannot be decompressed, is not valid JSON, or fails validation, THEN THE Horpang_App SHALL discard the decoded data, load the Default_Quiz, and display a visible message within 1 second stating that the shared quiz could not be loaded.
2. IF the URL fragment is longer than 8000 characters, THEN THE Horpang_App SHALL skip decoding, load the Default_Quiz, and display a visible message within 1 second stating that the link is too long.
3. IF decoding fails, THEN THE Share_Codec SHALL return an error value that identifies the failure as one of: decompression failure, invalid JSON, validation failure, or length limit exceeded. THE Share_Codec SHALL NOT throw an exception to the caller.
4. WHEN the Horpang_App displays a decoding failure message, THE Horpang_App SHALL render the message as text in the page, in an element exposed to screen readers as a live region, so that assistive technology announces it without requiring focus.
5. WHEN the Horpang_App loads the Default_Quiz after a decoding failure, THE Horpang_App SHALL allow the student to start and complete the Default_Quiz with no further error messages.
6. WHEN the student dismisses the failure message or starts the Default_Quiz, THE Horpang_App SHALL remove the failure message from the page.

## Area B: Interactive Horror UI

### Requirement 7: Title Stage

**User Story:** As a student, I want a dramatic blood-pool title screen, so that the game sets a spooky mood before I begin.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Title_Stage SHALL render a `#000000` background.
2. WHEN the Title_Stage finishes loading, THE Blood_Canvas SHALL spawn at least 6 blood pools arranged in a ring around the viewport center, one pool every 50 to 200 ms, and each pool SHALL grow from a point to its full size with an irregular, organic edge.
3. WHEN a blood pool spawns, THE Blood_Canvas SHALL spawn small splatter droplets around that pool.
4. WHEN the last pool of the ring has spawned, THE Blood_Canvas SHALL spawn one final pool at the viewport center after 1000 ms with a tolerance of 200 ms.
5. THE Blood_Canvas SHALL fill each blood pool with a deep crimson gradient running from `#8b0000` to `#320000` over the `#000000` background.
6. THE Title_Stage SHALL render the distressed HORPANG title in the Title_Font with a dark stroke, and SHALL reveal the title as the blood pools cover and frame it, so that the title is fully revealed within 4 seconds of the Title_Stage finishing loading.
7. IF the Title_Font fails to load, THEN THE Title_Stage SHALL render the HORPANG title as text in the fallback font stack (a bold system sans-serif or serif face), so that the title is readable.
8. WHEN the browser window is resized while the Title_Stage is shown, THE Blood_Canvas SHALL resize to the new viewport dimensions and redraw within 200 ms.
9. WHEN the Stage_Controller leaves the Title_Stage, THE Blood_Canvas SHALL stop its animation loop and release its animation frame request within 100 ms.
10. WHEN the HORPANG title is fully revealed, THE Title_Stage SHALL display the prompt "Click anywhere or press Enter to enter the dark..." and SHALL pulse its opacity between 0.4 and 1.0 on a 2-second cycle, animated by JavaScript.
11. WHEN the player clicks anywhere on the Title_Stage outside the corner button, or presses Enter while the Grimoire is closed, THE Stage_Controller SHALL advance to the Gallery_Stage within 500 milliseconds.
12. THE Title_Stage SHALL display a corner button labeled "Grimoire" in one corner of the viewport that is operable by mouse click, touch tap, and keyboard (Tab to focus, Enter or Space to activate).
13. WHEN the player activates the corner button, THE Title_Stage SHALL open the Grimoire within 500 milliseconds, SHALL keep the Title_Stage as the current stage, and SHALL NOT advance to the Gallery_Stage.
14. WHILE the Grimoire is open on the Title_Stage, THE Title_Stage SHALL ignore click-anywhere and Enter-to-advance input until the Grimoire is closed.
15. WHILE Lantern_Mode is on, THE Blood_Canvas SHALL spawn no more than half as many pools as the default count, SHALL grow each pool at no more than 50% of the default speed, and SHALL render no splatter droplet animation.
16. WHILE Lantern_Mode is on, THE Title_Stage SHALL pulse the prompt on a cycle of 4 seconds or longer with an opacity range no wider than 0.3 (for example 0.7 to 1.0).

### Requirement 8: Corridor Stage (Escape Door Hunt)

**User Story:** As a student, I want to search a dark corridor with a flashlight for the exit after clearing every photograph, so that escaping feels tense and earned.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Stage_Controller SHALL enter the Corridor_Stage only after every Question in the Quiz is solved in the Gallery_Stage.
2. WHEN the Corridor_Stage opens, THE Corridor_Stage SHALL render an original inline SVG stairwell scene fully covered by the Flashlight_Mask, with the only visible area being the lit center of the mask.
3. THE Flashlight_Mask SHALL be a shadow element of about 200vw by 200vh filled with a radial gradient that is transparent at the center and fades to `#000000` at about 20% of the gradient radius, and THE Corridor_Stage SHALL translate the element so that the transparent center follows the pointer or touch position.
4. WHILE the pointer moves over the Corridor_Stage, THE Flashlight_Mask SHALL center its light on the pointer position and update within 50 ms of each pointer movement.
5. WHILE the player touches and drags on the Corridor_Stage, THE Flashlight_Mask SHALL center its light on the touch position and update within 50 ms of each touch movement.
6. WHEN the Corridor_Stage opens, THE Corridor_Stage SHALL show the instruction "Move your flashlight to search the corridor and find the EXIT door..." for 5 seconds and then fade the instruction out over 1 second.
7. WHEN the Corridor_Stage opens, THE Corridor_Stage SHALL show the directions text "Find the door. Click to escape!" for the same 5 seconds and fade it out over the same 1 second as the instruction.
8. THE EXIT_Door SHALL be a button element with a transparent hit area positioned in the upper middle of the scene, a `#39ff14` glow, the visible text label "EXIT", and an accessible name of "EXIT door".
9. IF 8 seconds pass after the Corridor_Stage opens and the player has not clicked or tapped the EXIT_Door, THEN THE Corridor_Stage SHALL display a nudge message that points toward the EXIT_Door side of the screen and keep it visible until the player clicks or taps the EXIT_Door.
10. WHEN the player clicks or taps the EXIT_Door, THE Stage_Controller SHALL advance to the Summary_Stage within 1 second.
11. WHEN the EXIT_Door has keyboard focus and the player presses Enter or Space, THE Stage_Controller SHALL advance to the Summary_Stage within 1 second.
12. WHILE Lantern_Mode is on, THE Flashlight_Mask SHALL use a softer edge and a light radius at least 1.5 times the default radius.
13. IF the player clicks or taps a location on the Corridor_Stage outside the EXIT_Door, THEN THE Corridor_Stage SHALL remain on the Corridor_Stage and keep the current Flashlight_Mask state unchanged.

### Requirement 9: Gallery HUD

**User Story:** As a student, I want an always-visible status bar, so that I can track my score and progress.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE the Gallery_Stage is shown, THE HUD SHALL be visible at the top of the viewport, and THE HUD SHALL NOT be shown in the Title_Stage, Corridor_Stage or Summary_Stage. THE HUD SHALL display the score as a whole number from 0 to 999,999, the Streak_Multiplier, the Battery percentage, the progress text in the form "N / M Memories Banished" (where N is the count of banished memories and M is the total count of memories in the Gallery_Stage, with N never greater than M), and the Lantern_Mode toggle.
2. WHEN the Score_Engine state changes, THE HUD SHALL update the displayed score, Streak_Multiplier, Battery percentage, and progress text within 100 ms.
3. WHILE the Quiz_Modal is open, THE HUD SHALL remain visible and SHALL continue to display the current score, Streak_Multiplier, Battery percentage, and progress text without being covered by the Quiz_Modal.
4. THE HUD SHALL show the Battery value as a text percentage, an integer from 0 to 100 followed by "%", in addition to any graphical indicator.
5. IF the Score_Engine state is unavailable when the Gallery_Stage is shown, THEN THE HUD SHALL display a score of 0, a Battery percentage of 0%, and progress text with N equal to 0, and SHALL remain visible.
6. WHILE the Gallery_Stage is shown, THE Score_Footer SHALL display the current score at the bottom of the Gallery_Stage, remain visible while the page content scrolls and while the Quiz_Modal is open, and update within 100 ms of each score change so that it always equals the HUD score.

### Requirement 10: Polaroid Cards

**User Story:** As a student, I want spooky Polaroid photos that hide their questions until I inspect them, so that the gallery feels haunted.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Gallery_Stage loads a Quiz, THE Gallery_Stage SHALL render exactly one Polaroid for each Question in the Quiz, with no more and no fewer Polaroids than Questions.
2. THE Gallery_Stage SHALL lay out the Polaroids as a wrapping grid of inline cards, each with a `#666666` frame, padding and a drop shadow.
3. WHILE a Polaroid is neither hovered nor focused, THE Polaroid SHALL render with a grayscale filter (zero color saturation), a `blur(8px)` filter so that the Question text is not legible, a scale of 0.8, and a tilt rotation that alternates by position in the repeating sequence +5, -5, -3, +3 degrees, and the tilt SHALL remain fixed for the lifetime of that Polaroid instance and stay within -8 and +8 degrees inclusive.
4. WHILE a Polaroid is hovered or has keyboard focus, THE Polaroid SHALL render at 0 degrees rotation, scale 1 and no blur with the Question content legible, using a transition of 0.35 seconds, and WHEN hover and focus both end, THE Polaroid SHALL restore the tilt, scale and blur using the same 0.35-second transition.
5. THE Polaroid SHALL be a button element with an accessible label that includes the Question position and, for a solved Question, the text "Banished".
6. THE Polaroid SHALL show a Cursed_Photograph that is an original inline SVG generated by the Horpang_App, with no external image URL.
7. WHEN an unsolved Question's Polaroid is clicked, or has focus and receives the Enter key or the Space key, THE Gallery_Stage SHALL open the Quiz_Modal for that Question within 300 milliseconds.
8. WHEN a Question becomes solved, THE Polaroid SHALL display the Banished_Seal with the text "Banished" in the color `#39ff14` within 300 milliseconds.
9. WHEN a solved Question's Polaroid is clicked, or has focus and receives the Enter key or the Space key, THE Gallery_Stage SHALL keep the Quiz_Modal closed and SHALL leave the Polaroid's solved state unchanged.
10. THE Polaroid SHALL be reachable with the Tab key in document order, and WHILE a Polaroid has keyboard focus, THE Polaroid SHALL display a focus ring that is visible against the Gallery_Stage background.
11. IF the Quiz contains zero Questions, THEN THE Gallery_Stage SHALL render zero Polaroids and SHALL display a message indicating that no Questions are available.

### Requirement 11: Guidance Controller

**User Story:** As a student, I want clear instructions on what to do next, so that I never feel lost.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Gallery_Stage opens, THE Guidance_Controller SHALL select the banner text "Inspect a cursed photograph to uncover its question..." within 100 ms of the Gallery_Stage becoming visible.
2. WHEN the game state changes, THE Guidance_Controller SHALL select, within 100 ms of the change, the banner text for the new state from a fixed table of messages. The table has exactly one non-empty message for each of these 7 states: no questions solved, some questions solved, last unsolved question remaining, quiz modal open, after wrong answer, after correct answer, and all questions solved. The all-solved message SHALL direct the player to the corridor and the EXIT door (for example "All memories banished. Find the way out through the corridor...").
3. WHEN the Guidance_Controller is given the same game state more than once, THE Guidance_Controller SHALL return the same banner text each time, regardless of earlier states or elapsed time.
4. WHEN the Guidance_Controller selects new banner text, THE Gallery_Stage SHALL display it in a region that screen readers announce politely (an `aria-live="polite"` region), so that the announcement does not interrupt speech already in progress.
5. WHILE the Quiz_Modal is closed, THE Gallery_Stage SHALL display the guide banner, including the all-solved message until the Stage_Controller advances to the Corridor_Stage.
6. WHILE the Quiz_Modal is open, THE Gallery_Stage SHALL hide the guide banner from view.
7. IF the Guidance_Controller receives a game state that is not one of the 7 states in the message table, THEN THE Guidance_Controller SHALL keep the banner text currently displayed and SHALL NOT display an empty banner.

### Requirement 12: Quiz Modal

**User Story:** As a student, I want a focused question dialog, so that I can read and answer each question clearly.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Quiz_Modal opens, THE Quiz_Modal SHALL display the text "Question N of M", where N is the 1-based position of the current Question (1 to M) and M is the total number of Questions in the quiz, together with the Question prompt and every answer option as a selectable choice.
2. THE Quiz_Modal SHALL render bone text `#e8e4d8` on a black background (`#000000`) with a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for the prompt text, the answer option text and the "Question N of M" text.
3. WHILE the Quiz_Modal is open, WHEN the player presses Tab on the last focusable element in the dialog, THE Quiz_Modal SHALL move keyboard focus to the first focusable element in the dialog, and WHEN the player presses Shift+Tab on the first focusable element, THE Quiz_Modal SHALL move keyboard focus to the last focusable element in the dialog.
4. WHEN the player presses Escape and no answer is pending evaluation and no Jump_Scare is playing, THE Quiz_Modal SHALL close within 200 ms, discard any unsubmitted selection, and return keyboard focus to the Polaroid that opened it.
5. WHILE an answer is pending evaluation or a Jump_Scare is playing, THE Quiz_Modal SHALL ignore the Escape key, remain open, and leave keyboard focus unchanged.
6. WHEN the Quiz_Modal opens, THE Quiz_Modal SHALL move keyboard focus to the first answer option within 100 ms.
7. THE Quiz_Modal SHALL expose a dialog role with an accessible name, mark the dialog as modal, associate the Question prompt as the dialog's accessible description, expose the answer options as a labelled group of options each with an accessible name equal to its visible text, and expose the selected state of each option to screen readers.
8. IF the Question has no answer options, THEN THE Quiz_Modal SHALL display an error message indicating the Question cannot be answered, keep the dialog open, and move keyboard focus to the close control.

### Requirement 13: Correct Answer Rewards

**User Story:** As a student, I want rewards for right answers, so that I feel progress and stay motivated.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the player selects the correct option for an unsolved Question, THE Score_Engine SHALL add 100 multiplied by the Streak_Multiplier in effect before this answer to the score, and THE Quiz_Modal SHALL show the updated score within 500 ms.
2. WHEN the player selects the correct option for an unsolved Question, THE Score_Engine SHALL increment the Streak by 1.
3. WHEN the player selects the correct option for an unsolved Question, THE Score_Engine SHALL increase the Battery by 20, with a maximum resulting Battery value of 100.
4. WHEN the player selects the correct option for an unsolved Question, THE Score_Engine SHALL mark the Question as solved and increment the banished count by 1.
5. WHEN the player selects the correct option for an unsolved Question, THE Quiz_Modal SHALL display the Question study explanation within 500 ms, with a green cue and the text "Correct".
6. WHEN the player closes the study explanation, THE Quiz_Modal SHALL close within 500 ms and THE Polaroid SHALL display the Banished_Seal.
7. THE Score_Engine SHALL keep the banished count between 0 and the total number of Questions, and the Battery between 0 and 100, after every action.
8. IF the player selects the correct option for a Question that is already solved, THEN THE Score_Engine SHALL leave the score, Streak, Battery and banished count unchanged.

### Requirement 14: Wrong Answer and Jump Scare

**User Story:** As a student, I want wrong answers to be scary but instructive, so that the horror helps me remember the lesson.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the player selects an incorrect option and Lantern_Mode is off, THE Scare_Renderer SHALL display one full-screen Jump_Scare for 1500 ms with a tolerance of 200 ms.
2. WHEN the player selects an incorrect option, THE Score_Engine SHALL reset the Streak to 0 and decrease the Battery by 15 percentage points, with a minimum Battery value of 0.
3. WHEN the player selects an incorrect option, THE Score_Engine SHALL record the Question concept as a Missed_Concept, leave the Question unsolved, and record the concept only once if it is already recorded as a Missed_Concept.
4. WHEN the Jump_Scare ends, THE Quiz_Modal SHALL reappear within 300 ms with the Question study hint and the incorrect option marked with both a text label and an icon.
5. WHEN the player selects an incorrect option and Lantern_Mode is on, THE Quiz_Modal SHALL display the Question study hint and the incorrect option marked with both a text label and an icon within 300 ms, without displaying a Jump_Scare or the scare sting. The calm visual shake defined in Requirement 19 applies.
6. WHILE the Jump_Scare is playing, THE Horpang_App SHALL apply a screen shake to the viewport for the full duration of the Jump_Scare and SHALL stop the screen shake within 100 ms after the Jump_Scare ends.
7. WHEN the Jump_Scare starts, THE Audio_Engine SHALL play the scare sting once, starting within 100 ms of the Jump_Scare start.
8. THE Horpang_App SHALL fire a Jump_Scare only after an incorrect answer and SHALL leave correct answers and stage navigation without any Jump_Scare.
9. WHEN the player has already selected an incorrect option, THE Quiz_Modal SHALL disable that option, so that it cannot be selected again, for the remaining attempts on that Question.
10. IF the player selects an incorrect option while a Jump_Scare is already playing, THEN THE Horpang_App SHALL ignore the selection and SHALL leave the Streak, Battery, and Missed_Concept list unchanged.

### Requirement 15: Jump Scare Shuffler

**User Story:** As a student, I want varied scares, so that wrong answers stay surprising.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Scare_Shuffler SHALL select from exactly four Jump_Scares: Split-Face Mechanical Endoskeleton, Lunging Shadow Entity named "The Horpang", Analog CRT Static and Phantom Face, and Shattered Glass and Reaching Shadow Hands.
2. WHEN a wrong answer is submitted, THE Scare_Shuffler SHALL select one Jump_Scare that differs from the previously selected Jump_Scare.
3. WHEN the Scare_Shuffler makes its first selection in a session and no previous Jump_Scare exists, THE Scare_Shuffler SHALL select any one of the four Jump_Scares with equal probability of 25%.
4. FOR ALL sequences of 1000 selections with a seeded random source, THE Scare_Shuffler SHALL produce zero identical consecutive selections and SHALL select every Jump_Scare at least once.
5. WHEN the Split-Face Mechanical Endoskeleton is selected, THE Horpang_App SHALL display a faceplate that splits open along a vertical center line within 500 milliseconds to reveal glowing eyes and metallic teeth.
6. WHEN the Lunging Shadow Entity is selected, THE Horpang_App SHALL display a black silhouette with glowing crimson eyes that scales from 10% to at least 100% of the viewport height within 600 milliseconds, with a red and cyan chromatic aberration offset of 2 to 6 pixels.
7. WHEN the Analog CRT Static and Phantom Face is selected, THE Horpang_App SHALL display a high-contrast noise glitch that flashes a distorted monochrome ghostly face for 100 to 400 milliseconds.
8. WHEN the Shattered Glass and Reaching Shadow Hands is selected, THE Horpang_App SHALL display an SVG screen-crack overlay with at least two dark hands clawing in from the screen edges within 800 milliseconds.
9. THE Horpang_App SHALL build every Jump_Scare from original CSS and SVG, with no external image, video or audio files.
10. IF a Jump_Scare fails to render, THEN THE Horpang_App SHALL end the Jump_Scare within 1 second, show the wrong-answer feedback without it, and keep the student's quiz progress unchanged.

### Requirement 16: Summary Stage

**User Story:** As a student, I want a results screen at the end, so that I know how I did and what to study.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN all Questions in the Quiz are solved, THE Stage_Controller SHALL advance to the Corridor_Stage within 500 ms.
2. WHEN the EXIT_Door is activated in the Corridor_Stage, THE Stage_Controller SHALL enter the Summary_Stage as specified in Requirement 8.
3. WHEN the Summary_Stage is entered, THE Summary_Stage SHALL display the final score as the Score_Engine's total, together with the maximum possible score for the Quiz.
4. WHEN the Summary_Stage is entered, THE Summary_Stage SHALL display the accuracy percentage, calculated as the number of Questions answered correctly on the first attempt divided by the total number of Questions in the Quiz, multiplied by 100 and rounded half up to the nearest whole percent, in the range 0 to 100.
5. WHEN the Summary_Stage is entered, THE Summary_Stage SHALL list each Missed_Concept exactly once, ordered by the position of the first Question in the Quiz that referenced it, from first to last.
6. WHERE no Missed_Concept exists, THE Summary_Stage SHALL display a message stating that no concepts need review, and SHALL NOT display an empty Missed_Concept list.
7. WHEN the player activates the "Play Again" button by mouse click, touch, or by pressing Enter or Space while it has keyboard focus, THE Stage_Controller SHALL reset the Score_Engine to its initial state, mark every Question unsolved, clear all recorded Missed_Concepts and first-attempt results, and return to the Gallery_Stage with the same Quiz and the same Question order, within 500 ms.
8. WHEN the Summary_Stage is entered, THE Summary_Stage SHALL display the "Play Again" button, reachable by pressing the Tab key, and SHALL show a visible focus indicator while the button has keyboard focus.
9. IF the Summary_Stage cannot compute the score, accuracy, or Missed_Concept list, THEN THE Summary_Stage SHALL display an error message indicating that results are unavailable and SHALL still display an enabled "Play Again" button.

### Requirement 17: Educator Studio (The Grimoire)

**User Story:** As an educator, I want an in-app editor called The Grimoire, so that I can create and share my own quizzes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the educator opens the Grimoire, THE Grimoire SHALL display the current Quiz title and the list of Questions within 500 ms, showing an empty-list indicator if the Quiz has 0 Questions.
2. WHEN the educator activates the add control, THE Grimoire SHALL append one blank Question editor form to the end of the Quiz and move keyboard focus to the first field of that form.
3. IF the Quiz already contains the maximum number of Questions allowed by the Validator, THEN THE Grimoire SHALL disable the add control and display a message indicating the Question limit has been reached.
4. WHEN the educator changes the value of a Question field, THE Grimoire SHALL run the Validator and display the resulting errors adjacent to that field within 300 ms, and clear the errors for that field when the value becomes valid.
5. WHEN the educator activates the delete control for a Question, THE Grimoire SHALL display a confirmation prompt with confirm and cancel options, and the Quiz SHALL remain unchanged until the educator confirms.
6. WHEN the educator confirms the deletion of a Question, THE Grimoire SHALL remove that Question from the Quiz and update the displayed list.
7. WHEN the educator cancels the deletion prompt, THE Grimoire SHALL close the prompt and retain the Question unchanged.
8. WHEN the educator activates the JSON or CSV export control, THE Grimoire SHALL download the current Quiz as a file in the selected format, produced by the matching codec.
9. WHEN the educator selects a JSON or CSV file, THE Grimoire SHALL parse the file through the matching codec, replace the current Quiz with the result if parsing and validation succeed, and display the resulting errors without altering the current Quiz if they fail.
10. WHEN the educator activates "Generate Share Link", THE Grimoire SHALL display a URL containing the Share_Hash and a copy control.
11. WHEN the educator activates the copy control, THE Grimoire SHALL place the URL on the clipboard and display a confirmation within 500 ms, or display an error indicating the copy failed if the clipboard is unavailable.
12. IF the Quiz is invalid, THEN THE Grimoire SHALL disable the share and export controls and display the Validator errors.
13. IF the Share_Codec returns a size error, THEN THE Grimoire SHALL display the actual encoded length and the 8000-character limit, and SHALL NOT display a share URL.
14. WHEN the educator activates "Play This Quiz", THE Grimoire SHALL close and start the Title_Stage with the edited Quiz, provided the Quiz is valid.
15. THE Grimoire SHALL render all text with a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background, display a focus ring with at least 3:1 contrast on every focusable control, and allow every control to be operated by keyboard alone, using the black, bone and monochrome palette.

### Requirement 18: Audio Engine

**User Story:** As a student, I want procedurally generated sound, so that the game is atmospheric without any copyrighted media.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Audio_Engine SHALL synthesize all sounds at runtime using oscillators, noise sources, filters and gain envelopes, and SHALL NOT load or play any pre-recorded audio file.
2. WHEN the first user gesture (click, tap or key press) occurs, THE Audio_Engine SHALL create and start its AudioContext, and THE Audio_Engine SHALL NOT create or start the AudioContext before that gesture.
3. WHEN a Jump_Scare starts while Lantern_Mode is off and audio is not muted, THE Audio_Engine SHALL play a sting of 1.0 to 2.0 seconds total duration, made of a sub-bass drop (start frequency at most 120 Hz, falling) followed by a dissonant metallic screech (at least two simultaneous oscillators with a non-harmonic frequency ratio).
4. THE Audio_Engine SHALL limit the master gain value to a maximum of 0.5 on a 0.0 to 1.0 scale, and SHALL apply this limit to the sum of all concurrently playing sounds.
5. WHILE the Corridor_Stage or the Gallery_Stage is shown and audio is not muted, THE Audio_Engine SHALL play a generated ambient drone in a continuous loop with no silent gap longer than 100 ms.
6. WHILE the Title_Stage is shown, THE Audio_Engine SHALL remain silent except for UI sounds.
7. WHEN the user activates the mute control in the HUD or the Title_Stage, THE Audio_Engine SHALL silence all audio output within 100 ms, stop generating new sounds, and keep the muted state until the user activates the control again.
8. IF the browser does not support the Web Audio API, THEN THE Horpang_App SHALL continue all gameplay without sound, SHALL NOT show an error dialog or throw an uncaught exception, and SHALL keep the mute control visible and inert.
9. WHILE Lantern_Mode is on and audio is not muted, THE Audio_Engine SHALL play, when a Jump_Scare would otherwise start, a single soft tone of 0.2 to 0.6 seconds duration with a peak gain of at most 0.15, in place of the scare sting.

### Requirement 19: Lantern Mode

**User Story:** As a player who is sensitive to scares or motion, I want Lantern Mode, so that I can study without frightening or flashing effects.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the player activates the Lantern_Mode toggle, THE Lantern_Store SHALL save the new on or off setting to `localStorage` within 100 ms and apply it to the Horpang_App without requiring a page reload.
2. WHEN the Horpang_App starts and a saved Lantern_Mode setting exists, THE Horpang_App SHALL apply the saved setting before the first screen is interactive.
3. IF the Horpang_App starts and the saved Lantern_Mode value is not a valid on or off value, THEN THE Horpang_App SHALL discard the saved value and treat the setting as not saved.
4. WHEN the Horpang_App starts, no saved setting exists and the browser reports `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, THE Horpang_App SHALL turn Lantern_Mode on.
5. WHILE Lantern_Mode is on, THE Horpang_App SHALL replace each Jump_Scare with a calm visual shake of at most 400 ms and the study hint, and SHALL play no Jump_Scare sound or full-screen flash.
6. WHILE Lantern_Mode is on, THE Horpang_App SHALL emit no more than 3 luminance flashes in any one second period and SHALL disable strobing effects.
7. WHILE Lantern_Mode is on, THE Horpang_App SHALL reduce the motion of blood pools, splatter and pulses to a displacement of at most 4 px and an animation frequency of at most 1 cycle per second.
8. THE Horpang_App SHALL convey every green or crimson cue with text or an icon in addition to color.
9. IF `localStorage` is unavailable or a read or write to it throws an error, THEN THE Lantern_Store SHALL keep the setting in memory for the current session, THE Horpang_App SHALL continue without displaying an error, and the setting SHALL revert to the default on the next start.
10. THE Lantern_Mode toggle SHALL be operable with the Enter and Space keys, SHALL expose its on or off state to screen readers, and SHALL display a visible focus ring with a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 against the adjacent background.

### Requirement 20: No External Assets

**User Story:** As a developer, I want every visual to be original and self-contained, so that the game has no licensing risk and works offline.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Horpang_App SHALL render every Cursed_Photograph, the Corridor_Stage stairwell background and every Jump_Scare illustration as original inline SVG or CSS.
2. THE Horpang_App SHALL reference no third-party image, video or audio URL.
3. THE Horpang_App SHALL load the Google_Fonts_Stylesheet as the only external resource, to supply the Title_Font and other display fonts, each with a system fallback font stack.
4. IF the Google_Fonts_Stylesheet fails to load, THEN THE Horpang_App SHALL render all text in the fallback font stack and SHALL remain fully playable through every stage.
5. WHILE the browser has no network connection, THE Horpang_App SHALL remain fully playable from the Title_Stage to the Summary_Stage using the already loaded application files.
